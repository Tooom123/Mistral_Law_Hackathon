      The pipeline

      The LLM reads. The code judges.
      The lawyer decides.

      1. 01ReadOCR and vision: every fact keeps its page and position.
      2. 02RebuildActs, times, actors: one timeline per category.
      3. 03CheckRules as code, using the law in force on the act’s date.
      4. 04Cross-checkTwo reports, two different times for the same fact.
      5. 05CascadeWhat falls with the act: the acts to name in the motion.
      6. 06GroundArticle text and case law retrieved from Judilibre.
      7. 07ActThe deadline to watch and the grounds to examine.

      Every finding answers four questions

      Not a summary.
      A flaw, with its page number.

      What

      A fact, with no legal conclusion: rights were notified well after custody began.

      Where

      Document, page, highlighted line. One click opens the source page.

      Why

      The rule in its applicable version, the acts potentially affected, the precedents.

      What next

      What the lawyer must check — prejudice, a justification in the file — and the deadline.

      Guardrails

      An audit tool,
      not an oracle.

      • No source, no finding. Everything on screen points to a page in the file.
      • No made-up percentages. Three levels: documented, inferred, needs reading.
      • No nullity declared. We speak of “grounds to examine”; prejudice is for the lawyer to assess.
      • No hallucinated case law. Only decisions retrieved from the Judilibre API are cited.
      • Both sides of the bar. The same audit serves the defense and the prosecutor who wants a clean procedure.
