      The pipeline

      The model reads. The code cross-checks.
      The lawyer decides.

      1. 01ReadOCR and vision: every fact keeps its page and position.
      2. 02LinkPeople, companies, decisions, declarations: one graph for the whole case.
      3. 03Cross-checkOne statement, two documents, two versions of the same fact.
      4. 04SpotSomeone in a decision, with a past tie to the company that benefited.
      5. 05TraceFrom the decision to the claim, then to the original evidence.
      6. 06WeighWhat becomes problematic if this information is wrong.
      7. 07ActThe points to put in front of a reviewer, with the pages.

      Every finding answers four questions

      Not a summary.
      A conflict of interest, with the pages.

      What

      A plain fact: person X took part in decision Y, and company Z — a former client — benefited from it.

      Where

      Three documents, three pages, the lines highlighted. One click opens the source.

      Why

      The whole chain: person, role, decision, benefiting company, and what depends on each link.

      What next

      What to verify — declaration of interests, dates, actual role — and who should do it.

      Guardrails

      An audit tool,
      not an oracle.

      • No source, no finding. Everything on screen points to a page in the case file.
      • No made-up percentages. Three levels: documented, inferred, needs reading.
      • No conflict declared. We say “potential conflict of interest — to review”; the legal qualification belongs to a lawyer.
      • No invented relationship. Person–company links come from the documents; an inferred link is labeled as such.
      • For anyone who audits. Inquiry commissions, audit firms, compliance teams, legal departments, lawyers: the same audit.
